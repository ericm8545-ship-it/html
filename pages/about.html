About Urban Paws

Happy Pets. Happier Lives.

Our Story

Born From a Love for Four-Legged Family

Founded in Dublin, Urban Paws began with a simple observation: busy pet owners struggled to find trustworthy grooming, holistic care, and quality accessories in one welcoming place.

What started as a small neighbourhood grooming studio has grown into a modern pet care hub. Today, we combine gentle, stress-free grooming with thoughtfully curated treats and everyday essentials—designed for owners who treat their pets as part of the family.

Our Mission

To deliver reliable, gentle, and accessible pet grooming alongside nutritious treats and durable accessories—making everyday pet care effortless for owners and joyful for pets.

Our Core Values

Everything we do at Urban Paws is guided by four promises to you and your pet.

Gentle, Fear-Free Care

Every dog is treated with patience, calm handling, and positive reinforcement—never rushed.

Natural & Safe Products

From hypoallergenic shampoos to healthy treats, we vet every ingredient for safety and quality.

Trusted Expertise

Our certified groomers and pet wellness specialists continuously train in breed-specific care.

Community First

We build lasting relationships with local pet owners, supporting adoption drives and responsible pet ownership.
